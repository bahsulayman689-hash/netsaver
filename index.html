Privacy

What this app stores and shares
Stays on your phone: bundles, usage, top-ups, notes, connection logs and test results. Sent only when you choose: a report or a shared bundle price. Reports are reviewed before they are published, locations are rounded to about 1 km, and phone-number-like text is hidden. Please do not write names or phone numbers in reports. To remove data from this phone, clear the site data in your browser settings. To ask for a published report to be removed, contact the person who runs this app.
